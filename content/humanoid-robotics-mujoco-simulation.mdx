When simulating bipedal humanoids, the gap between abstract mathematical kinematics and physical stability is razor-thin. For the Menlo Asimov 1 humanoid robot, realistic testing requires zero compromises on collision geometry, actuator latency, and contact friction.

## Why MuJoCo for Humanoid Physics?

MuJoCo (Multi-Joint dynamics with Contact) excels at resolving convex contact dynamics and tendon constraints with high numerical fidelity. In building **VASIMOV**, our primary goal was to provide an exact digital twin that obeys the official Asimov wire protocol while streaming live ground-truth telemetry.

### The 78-to-23 Policy Pipeline

The official Asimov 1 locomotion policy consumes a 78-dimensional observation vector at 50 Hz:

- Base orientation quaternion and angular velocity
- Simulated 6-axis IMU acceleration readings
- 25 joint positions and joint angular velocities
- History buffer of previous target actions

The neural network outputs 23 PD actuator target angles, mapped through torque limits to the robot's high-torque brushless DC joints.

## Bridging MuJoCo to the Browser

While MuJoCo runs natively in C/Python, operators need fluid, browser-based inspection. Rather than streaming heavy video frames, **VASIMOV** streams lightweight binary joint state vectors over WebSockets. A Three.js client decodes the kinematic transform tree directly on the client GPU, delivering 60 FPS visualization with sub-15ms network latency.

This architecture enables:
1. **Interactive joint overrides**: Operators can inject manual joint biases while the underlying walking policy maintains base balance.
2. **Procedural obstacle stress-testing**: Spawning steps, slopes, and randomized friction patches on the fly.
3. **Flight Data Recording (FDR)**: Replaying joint slip incidents frame-by-frame for post-mortem analysis.
