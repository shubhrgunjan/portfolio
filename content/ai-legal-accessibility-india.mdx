In India, navigating civil rights, consumer disputes, tenancy laws, or government grievance mechanisms is rarely a question of willingness — it is a problem of legal jargon and linguistic barriers.

At the **Build in AI for India Hackathon (Delhi Edition)**, we built **Nyaya Flow** to solve this: allowing a person to speak their problem in their everyday native language and receive an immediate, actionable, step-by-step roadmap grounded in verified legal statutes.

## The Technical Pipeline

Nyaya Flow brings together three key technologies:

1. **Gnani.ai Speech Recognition**: Accepts spoken vernacular voice inputs (Hindi, English, regional dialects), transcribing raw audio even in noisy environments.
2. **Context Formulation & Legal Extraction**: Identifies the fundamental dispute type (e.g. unlawful eviction, non-refund of deposit, consumer grievance).
3. **Structured Action Schema (Google Gemini)**: Formats the legal guidance into four distinct, non-ambiguous phases:
   - Immediate documentation to gather (receipts, notices, agreements)
   - Pre-litigation formal communications (legal notice drafts)
   - Relevant statutory bodies or online portals (e.g., e-Daakhil, RERA, National Consumer Helpline)
   - Escalation procedures and legal aid clinics.

```typescript
// Sample action pathway generation contract
interface LegalActionRoadmap {
  disputeCategory: string;
  relevantActs: string[];
  immediateSteps: { stepNumber: number; action: string; requiredDocuments: string[] }[];
  officialPortals: { name: string; url: string; freeOfCost: boolean }[];
  statutoryNoticeDraftTemplate?: string;
}
```

By ensuring that every step is source-backed and explicitly transparent about legal boundaries, Nyaya Flow bridges the gap between everyday citizens and institutional justice.
